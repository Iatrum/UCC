## Contract and decisions
- Device identifier `clinic|<current clinic>` AND type coding `https://ucc.emr/poct/analyser-type|<catalog analyserType>`. Device.property.type coding `https://ucc.emr/poct/device-property|last-heartbeat`; valueCode[0].text contains ISO timestamp. Active + timestamp age 0..90s = online; missing/invalid/future/stale/inactive = offline. These are explicit UCC conventions, not Medplum built-ins. GET follows all clinic-filtered pages and returns only minimal device summaries. Sign revalidates device, capability, patient and encounter clinic before writes.
- Simulated devices are real FHIR Devices tagged `https://ucc.emr/poct|simulated`, accepted only outside production in allowlisted fake clinics demo/clinicb/clinicc/clinicd. No synthetic always-online response, no automatic production fallback. E2E creates and removes fake devices.
- Draft metadata adds kind=poct, code, analyserId and analyserName; existing Record<string,string> drafts stay valid. Catalog prices are provisional demo prices. No result inputs, flags or warning banners.
- POCT and existing lab lines are excluded from completed Procedures. Their original charge fields persist in an Encounter extension as billingItems, consumed by existing checkout/bill mapping. MC/medication/service behavior stays on its current path. POCT orders are created before other Sign writes, errors preserve the draft, and a clinic/entry identifier prevents duplicate POCT orders on retry.
