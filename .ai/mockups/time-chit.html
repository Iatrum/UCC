Design notes: layout, letterhead treatment, rule lines, patient box and signature/stamp block are deliberately identical to the existing MC template (lib/document-templates.ts / components/mc/mc-document.tsx) so staff already familiar with issuing an MC recognise the Time Chit flow immediately. The only structural addition is the three-cell time-in/time-out/duration row, and the explicit "not a Medical Certificate" disclaimer — both called for in .ai/research/time-chit.md §2 so the document can't be mistaken for, or misused as, a certification of unfitness for work. Colours/typography (#1e3a5f primary, Helvetica/Arial) match the clinic letterhead variables already in lib/document-templates.ts.
